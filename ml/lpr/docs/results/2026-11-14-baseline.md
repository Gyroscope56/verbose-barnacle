# Synthetic LPR baseline, 14 November 2026

This is a controlled printed-plate baseline using synthetic Wisconsin, Illinois, and Minnesota-inspired artwork. All training and reported synthetic evaluation images are generated locally. No Unidata or LPLCv2 images are included. Real webcam evaluation and UNO Q execution remain unverified.

The dataset contains 10,000 full scenes, including 802 plate-free scenes, and 30,000 OCR crops. Scene partitions contain 8,000 training, 1,000 validation, and 1,000 test images. OCR partitions contain 24,176 training, 2,918 validation, and 2,906 test crops. Plate strings are disjoint across partitions. Backgrounds are procedural indoor and vehicle scenes; they do not establish accuracy on real vehicle photographs.

Training uses an Apple M4 Pro with 24 GB memory, Python 3.13.6, PyTorch 2.15.0, Ultralytics 8.4.213, and MPS. OCR uses native MPS CTC loss. Software versions are recorded in the lock file; seeds are recorded, but upstream MPS operators warn that some training reductions are not bitwise deterministic.

OCR completed 20 epochs in 375.5 seconds. Its best validation exact-string accuracy was 90.68% on the 2,918 held-out synthetic crops. Detector training uses YOLO11 nano initialized from public pretrained weights. The detector was resumed from completed epoch 2 to enforce the user's hard cap of 12 epochs after confirming Ultralytics time mode expands its schedule.

End-to-end accuracy counts every positive scene, including any missed or misplaced detection. Matches require IoU >= 0.5 and exact normalized plate text. Detection uses a 0.25 confidence threshold. Ground-truth-box crops and predicted-box crops differ slightly in their borders, so their recognition scores need not be ordered. The full OCR-crop set also includes additional smaller rendered scenes and crop jitter; it is reported separately.

Latency uses 50 decoded 640 by 480 images, batch size one, five warm-up calls, MPS synchronization, and detector size 640. It includes preprocessing and postprocessing and excludes disk reads, camera capture, and network work. Training was finished before measurement. These Mac timings do not establish UNO Q latency or camera throughput.

Checkpoints were selected using validation only. No settings or weights were tuned against these test results. The failure sheet includes O/0 substitutions and missed or extra characters; harder images remain the largest recognition limitation. Confidence scores are uncalibrated.

## Export and independent example

Both final selected models were exported to fixed batch-one ONNX opset 18. CPU ONNX Runtime outputs matched PyTorch on six generated validation scenes/crops with rtol=0.001 and atol=0.001. Maximum absolute differences were 0.00004578 for OCR logits and 0.00057983 for detector outputs. OCR decoded text matched in all six comparisons.

A fresh synthetic Wisconsin-style `ABC1234` scene, whose text was verified absent from the training manifest, was detected and read correctly by the saved models. This is an independent synthetic example, not a real-camera validation.

ONNX export parity verifies portable raw model outputs only. Host NMS, coordinate mapping, and CTC decoding are still required for a standalone ONNX pipeline; UNO Q execution and acceleration have not been tested.
